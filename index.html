02 // REAL-TIME VISION AI

Gemini Live watches through your camera in real time. Walking through the gym, it identifies every machine, assesses what is available, and builds an optimised workout on the fly — no app, no pre-set plan, just a live conversation.

GEMINI LIVE — GYM SESSION // REAL-TIME VISION DEMO
VISION · AUDIO · LIVE
GEMINI LIVE REAL-TIME CAMERA EQUIPMENT RECOGNITION WORKOUT PLANNING VOICE FEEDBACK
01 // SEES
Gemini Live watches through your phone camera in real time — identifying machines, free weights, cables, and open floor space as you walk through the gym.
02 // THINKS
It cross-references what is available against your goals — building a push/pull split, estimating time, and suggesting exercise order to maximise efficiency.
03 // TALKS

04 // AI AS YOUR WEB DEVELOPER

A real website. Real feedback. Real gym photos. Watch what happens when you hand AI an outdated government site, describe what you want, and let it build something from scratch — no agency, no developer, no budget.

THE STARTING POINT
An existing fitness society website — functional but visually dated, built on an old template, and no longer reflecting the community it serves.
THE INPUTS TO AI
The original site as reference  ·  Member feedback on what was missing  ·  Photos of the actual gym  ·  Plain English description of the desired look and feel
THE OUTPUT
A fully coded, modern website — responsive, dark-themed, with real gym photography, a booking flow, and clean navigation. Built in a single session.
